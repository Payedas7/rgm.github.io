Data deletion

Request deletion of your account and data

Use this page to ask us to delete your Radha Gopinath Mandir (IHF NJNY) account data.

Send your request from the email tied to your account so we can verify ownership.

How to submit a deletion request

  1. Email us at info@ihf-njny.org with subject “Data Deletion Request”.
  2. Include the details below to help us verify and process your request:
  • Full name
  • Email address used in the app (required)
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Any family/child profiles linked to your account
  • What you want deleted (entire account, specific profiles, practice logs, reminders)

We may ask for confirmation from the registered email to verify ownership.

What will be deleted

  • Account profile and linked family/child profiles
  • Practice goals and tracking history (sādhanā, svādhyāya, seva)
  • Notification/reminder settings and device tokens
  • Active sessions and authentication tokens

What may be retained

  • Minimal audit logs required for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance
  • Records we are legally required to keep (if any); these are minimized and retained only as long as required

Timeline

We aim to process deletion requests within 30 days of verifying your identity.
